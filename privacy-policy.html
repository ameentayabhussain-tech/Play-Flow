Play Store Compliance
Version 1.0.0  •  Google Play Edition (30 MB)

Updated: November 2026. This Privacy Policy is specifically optimized for PlayFlow v1.0.0 users on the Google Play Store, detailing how we protect your personal media and respect Play Store policies.

1. PRIVACY-FIRST ARCHITECTURE & AUTHENTICATION

Play Flow is engineered around a local-first philosophy. Your local video files, audio tracks, photos, and personal media remain strictly on your device in private sandboxed storage.

  • Local Media Indexing: Song metadata, video play history, custom playlists, equalizer presets, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using Isar and SQLite.
  • Zero Automatic Media Uploads: Play Flow never automatically uploads your personal videos, music, or Private Vault files to remote servers.
  • Anonymous & Google Sign-In: Guest sessions automatically assign an anonymous Firebase ID to protect referral rewards and settings. Signing in with Google links your account securely without inspecting or transmitting local media files.

2. PLAYFLOW KIDS CINEMA & CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA / GDPR KIDS)

Play Flow includes dedicated child-safe media environments tailored for young users on Google Play:

  • 100% On-Device Content Filtering: Video titles in Kids Cinema are analyzed locally using cartoon keyword matching to ensure safe, wholesome playback.
  • Zero Child Data Harvesting: Play Flow does not request, collect, harvest, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children.
  • Parental Controls & Screen Time Limits: Parents can configure age thresholds and daily screen limits saved locally in secure SharedPreferences.
  • COPPA Compliance: PlayFlow Kids Cinema strictly complies with COPPA regulations on the Play Store by functioning without targeted ads for children.

3. CURATED FAMILY & EDUCATIONAL CONTENT HUB

PlayFlow provides access to an optional curated content directory featuring family-friendly, educational, and cultural media streams:

  • Pre-Approved Safe Channels: The Curated Hub displays strictly whitelisted educational, STEM, nature, and family content vetted for wholesome viewing.
  • Sandboxed Player Protection: Streams play inside a locked, sandboxed player environment with external third-party web tracking, cookies, and unmonitored browsing disabled.

4. TASK CENTER & PLAYFLOW COIN ECONOMY

Play Flow includes a virtual reward system powered by PlayFlow Coins:

  • Reward Tracking: PlayFlow Coins earned through Task Center milestones or rewarded ad views are stored locally and synced with your anonymous account ID.
  • Financial Privacy: Play Flow does not collect financial details or bank accounts for coin accumulation.

5. VIDEO DOWNLOADER & MEDIA UTILITIES

The built-in Video Downloader and media management tools operate with total transparency:

  • Local Direct Storage: Downloads are saved directly to public Android MediaStore or chosen local folders.
  • Halal Guard Inspection: URLs and file names are analyzed on-device by Halal Guard to prevent accessing objectionable media.

6. PRIVATE VAULT & AES-256 ENCRYPTION

The Private Folder offers maximum security for confidential local files:

  • Hardware Biometric Security: Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face unlock) is handled exclusively by hardware Android KeyStore.
  • Zero Password Recovery: Encryption keys stay on-device. Passcodes are not stored on remote servers.

7. SYSTEM PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED

Play Flow requests standard Android permissions strictly to power core app features:

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required for local library indexing, Duplicate Finder, and Storage Doctor.
  • Background Audio: Enables uninterrupted playback when the screen is locked.
  • Notifications: Displays active playback controls and real-time conversion/download progress.

8. OPTIONAL CLOUD HUB INTEGRATIONS

Play Flow supports optional backup and streaming via Google Drive and Microsoft OneDrive:

  • User-Initiated Sync: Cloud file uploads and downloads only take place when explicitly requested by you.
  • Secure Token Management: OAuth tokens are saved in Android EncryptedSharedPreferences and can be disconnected at any time.

9. ADVERTISING NETWORKS & ANALYTICS

To keep core features free and ensure software stability, we utilize industry-standard SDKs for Google Play:

  • Google AdMob: Integrated as the primary advertising network for Google Play Store distributions.
  • Advertising Identifiers: AdMob uses non-sensitive Advertising Identifiers (GAID) to serve context-appropriate ads.
  • Crash Diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics gathers anonymous crash reports and hardware specs to help us eliminate bugs.

10. USER CONTROL & DATA RIGHTS

You retain complete control over your data:

  • History & Cache Management: Clear playback history or thumbnail caches whenever you choose in Settings.
  • GDPR & CCPA Rights: You may request data deletion by clearing app storage in Android System Settings or contacting support.

11. CONTACT US

If you have questions, feedback, or privacy concerns regarding Play Flow on the Google Play Store, please reach out to our privacy team at playflowsupport@gmail.com.
