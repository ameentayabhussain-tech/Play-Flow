Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Play Flow Video Player is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

• Personal Data: When you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture to personalize your experience and enable optional cloud backup. • Media Files: We request access to device storage so the app can play your videos, audio, and images. Media files are processed locally on your device unless you choose to use cloud backup features. • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage information such as playback behavior, feature usage, and app performance to improve the app experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: • Authenticate users securely with Google Sign-In. • Provide local playback, file management, and optional cloud backup. • Enable background audio playback and media conversion services. • Improve app stability and optimize performance for low-end devices.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to support app functionality: • Google Play Services: for app distribution and updates. • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: for secure login and optional cloud data storage. • Google Sign-In: for streamlined account access. • FFmpeg Kit: for media conversion and processing. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies, and we recommend reviewing them.

4. Data Security

• Secure Vault: Files moved to the private vault are encrypted using AES-256. • Local Processing: Playback and conversion happen on-device whenever possible to maximize privacy. • Recycle Bin: Deleted files are retained in a hidden internal directory for 20 days before permanent deletion.

5. Permissions

To provide full functionality, the app may request these permissions: • Storage / Media Access: Required for playing and organizing files on your device. On Android 11+, All Files Access is used only where needed for deep folder access. • Foreground Service: Required to keep audio playing while the app is in the background. • Notifications: Used for playback controls in the notification shade. • Biometrics: Optional, used to secure the Private Folder with fingerprint or face unlock. • Internet: Used for optional features such as Google Sign-In and cloud backup.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: • Access, update, or delete your personal information. • Revoke app permissions through your device settings. • Request account deletion, which removes your data from our Firebase services where applicable.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Please review this page regularly for any changes. Continued use of the app after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@playflow.com.
