Privacy Policy
✓ Your Privacy is Protected

Last Updated: 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Play Flow handles your data, system permissions, video downloader, Kids Cinema, and cloud features in a transparent and secure manner.

1. Privacy-First Architecture & Authentication

Play Flow is built with a local-first philosophy. Your local audio, video, and personal media files remain strictly stored on your device in private sandboxed storage.

  • Local Processing: Song metadata, video history, playlists, equalizer profiles, and custom preferences are indexed locally on your device using Isar/SQLite.
  • Zero Automatic Uploads: Play Flow never automatically uploads your personal videos, songs, or private vault files to external servers or third parties.
  • Anonymous & Google Sign-In: Guest sessions automatically assign an anonymous Firebase ID to preserve your referral rewards and app preferences. Upgrading to Google Sign-In links your account seamlessly without transmitting personal files.
  • Student Pass & Referral Protection: Verification emails and single-use invite codes are securely processed via Firebase Firestore and protected against unauthorized data scraping.

2. PlayFlow Kids Cinema & Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR Kids)

Play Flow includes a specialized, child-safe media viewing surface called PlayFlow Kids Cinema:

  • 100% Local Content Filtering: Video titles selected in Kids Cinema are analyzed on-device using local cartoon keyword matching to ensure safe, wholesome cartoon playback (e.g. Motu Patlu, Peppa Pig, Doraemon, Tom & Jerry, Cocomelon).
  • No Children Data Collection: Play Flow does not request, store, harvest, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or biometric data from children or minor users.
  • Parental Controls & Daily Screen Limits: Parents can configure age thresholds (under 15 years) and daily screen time limits (2h/3h) stored locally in SharedPreferences.
  • COPPA & GDPR Compliance: PlayFlow Kids Cinema complies with COPPA and GDPR Kids guidelines by operating completely offline without ad tracking targeted at children.

3. Video Downloader & Media Tool Privacy

The built-in Video Downloader and media management tools operate with strict local transparency:

  • Local Direct Downloads: Video downloads are saved directly to your device storage in "DCIM/PlayFlow_Downloads" or public MediaStore.
  • No Downloader Tracking: Downloaded URLs, media filenames, and progress statistics remain strictly local and are never logged on remote servers.
  • Halal Guard Inspection: URLs and filenames are scanned on-device by Halal Guard to prevent accessing harmful or unethical content.

4. System Permissions Explained

Play Flow requests standard Android system permissions solely to enable core features, media tools, and playback enhancers:

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) & Media Permissions: Required for library scanning, Media Manager, Duplicate Finder, Format Converter, Video Compressor, Video Downloader, Storage Doctor, and Recycle Bin.
  • Background Audio & Battery Optimization Exemption: Allows uninterrupted background audio and video playback when the screen is turned off or when switching apps.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Displays ongoing media playback controls and real-time conversion/download progress bars in the Android notification shade.
  • Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used exclusively to secure access to your local Private Vault. Biometric data stays hardware-isolated on your device.
  • Camera & Microphone (CAMERA / RECORD_AUDIO): Used strictly when capturing live video clips or recording voiceovers inside the Video Editor tool.
  • Network & Wi-Fi Permissions: Utilized for TV Casting (Chromecast/DLNA), Subtitle Downloader, Network Discovery, Video Downloader, and optional Cloud Hub sync.

5. Optional Cloud Hub Integrations

Play Flow supports optional backup and streaming via Google Drive and OneDrive:

  • User-Controlled Sync: File uploads and downloads only occur when explicitly triggered by you in Cloud Hub.
  • Encrypted Storage: Media files stored in the Private Vault are encrypted with AES-256 before cloud backup.
  • Secure OAuth Tokens: Authentication tokens are stored securely in Android EncryptedSharedPreferences/KeyStore and can be revoked at any time.

6. On-Device Content Filtering (Halal Guard)

The Halal Guard tool provides automated ethical content filtering for a safer media viewing experience:

  • 100% Local Evaluation: Keywords and media filter rules are processed entirely on-device.
  • Strict Confidentiality: Filter settings and blocked media logs are stored locally and never transmitted anywhere.

7. Advertising Networks & Analytics

To support our free tier and ensure application stability across build flavors, we integrate trusted ad networks and diagnostics SDKs:

  • Flavor-Specific Ad Networks: Google AdMob is utilized strictly in PlayStore builds. Unity Ads network is utilized exclusively in Global and alternative store builds (Amazon / Samsung).
  • Anonymous Identifiers: Ad networks utilize anonymous Advertising Identifiers (GAID) to serve non-sensitive ads. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your Android System Settings.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Sentry: Collect anonymous crash logs and hardware specs to help us fix bugs and improve performance.

8. User Control & Data Rights

You retain complete control over your data in Play Flow:

  • History & Cache Management: Clear playback history or purge temporary thumbnail caches anytime in Settings.
  • Cloud Disconnect: Unlink Google Drive or OneDrive with one tap to immediately delete local credentials.
  • GDPR & CCPA Rights: Request data exports or completely delete app data by logging out or clearing app storage in Android System Settings.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or privacy concerns regarding Play Flow, please reach out to our team at support@playflow.app.
