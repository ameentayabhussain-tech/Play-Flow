Privacy Policy
✓ Trust Protocol 3.0

Last Updated: November 15, 2026. This Privacy Policy details our data processing practices, permission usage for all tools, and your rights as a user.

1. Privacy-First Local Architecture

Play Flow operates primarily on a local, device-centric architecture. Data operations are performed locally in your device's sandboxed environment whenever possible.

  • Local Media Metadata: Song titles, video paths, artwork, play counts, and playback position are indexed and stored locally in Isar/SQLite databases on your device.
  • Identity Information: When you choose to log in with Google, we receive basic identity details (email and display name) solely to identify your account for Cloud Hub sync.
  • Zero Third-Party Media Uploads: Your local audio and video files are never automatically uploaded to any third-party server or external database.

2. Comprehensive Tools & Permissions Explanation

To provide advanced media playback, file management, and background playback features, Play Flow requests specific system permissions.

  • All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) & Storage Permissions: Essential for core tools like Duplicate Finder, Media Manager, Storage Doctor, File Converter, and Recycle Bin to locate, organize, clean, and restore media across storage directories.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): Requested to allow the background AudioService to run uninterrupted without being prematurely terminated by Android's power saver routines.
  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display ongoing playback notification controls in the Android shade and LockScreen widget.
  • Foreground Services (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Maintains real-time media session controls and active audio pipeline when screen is off.
  • Camera & Microphone Permissions: Utilized exclusively within the Video Editor & GIF Creator tools when importing live camera footage or recording voiceovers.
  • Biometric Permission (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used solely to authenticate access to your Private Folder / Vault. Biometric readings stay hardware-isolated and are never transmitted.
  • Network & Wi-Fi Permissions: Required for TV Casting (DLNA/Chromecast), Subtitle Downloader, Network Discovery/Speed Test, and Cloud Hub synchronization.

3. Cloud Hub & Backup Integrations

Play Flow integrates with third-party cloud providers (Google Drive API and Microsoft Graph API for OneDrive) for optional off-site backup.

  • User-Initiated Backup: Uploads and downloads occur strictly under your explicit direction within Cloud Hub.
  • Encryption before Sync: Media files stored inside the Private Folder are encrypted with AES-256 before any cloud backup transaction.
  • Revocable Tokens: Cloud authentication tokens are stored securely using EncryptedSharedPreferences/KeyStore and can be revoked at any time.

4. Ethical Filtering (Halal Guard)

The Halal Guard tool offers automated content filtering to block specified keywords or media types based on ethical preferences.

  • 100% On-Device Analysis: Title inspection and rule evaluation occur completely on your device.
  • Privacy Assurance: Filtered keywords, blocked video logs, and preference rules are stored in isolated local preference files and never uploaded anywhere.

5. Advertising, Analytics & Third-Party SDKs

We integrate trusted SDKs to support our free tier and improve application reliability.

  • Google AdMob & AppLovin MAX: Serve contextual ads using anonymous Advertising Identifiers (GAID). You can reset your Advertising ID via Android System Settings.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Sentry: Collect anonymous crash reports, stack traces, and hardware parameters to help us resolve crashes.
  • User Tracking Transparency: We respect App Tracking Transparency choices and honor Android opt-outs for personalized ads.

6. Granular User Controls

You maintain full authority over how Play Flow handles your data and settings.

  • History Controls: Enable/disable playback history logging or wipe your entire history from the History screen.
  • Cache Management: Instantly purge decrypted temporary files, thumbnail caches, and temporary subtitles in Settings or Storage Doctor.
  • Cloud Disconnect: Unlink Google Drive or OneDrive in one click, immediately deleting local OAuth access tokens.
  • Permission Toggles: You can revoke individual Android permissions (e.g., Battery Exemption, Notifications) anytime via Android App Info settings.

7. Global User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We extend standard GDPR and CCPA privacy protections to all Play Flow users regardless of region.

  • Right to Access & Portability: Request an export of your locally saved app preferences and database records.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): Log out and clear app data to completely purge local and cloud-linked user profiles.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of personalized advertising directly through device system settings.
  • Contact Privacy Team: For privacy queries or data requests, email privacy@playflow.app.

8. Children's Privacy

Play Flow is designed for general audiences (13+). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
